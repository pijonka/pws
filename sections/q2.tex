\subsection{Analysis \& conclusion}
The results yield the following pattern: fragments A through C increase in perceived enjoyment (from $2.25$ to $3.28$); fragment D decreases (to $2.81$); fragments D through F stagnate almost completely (fragments D and E are both congruent at $2.81$; fragment F increases negligibly with $0.05$); fragments G plummets (to $1.19$); and, fragments H through J are in a range (between $2.94$ and $3.39$) that fragment C also fits into.
